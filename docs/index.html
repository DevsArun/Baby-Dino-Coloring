Privacy Policy — Baby Dino Coloring

Effective date: August 2026

Baby Dino Coloring (package com.itschool.babydinocoloring) is a children's coloring app published by It School on the Amazon Appstore.

We collect nothing

This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or data of any kind. Specifically, the app has:

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No user accounts, login, or registration
  • No internet permission — the app works fully offline
  • No access to camera, microphone, location, or contacts

Drawings stay on the device

Coloring progress is saved only in the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

In-app purchase

The app offers one optional, one-time purchase (unlocking all coloring pages) processed entirely by the Amazon Appstore. We never see or store any payment information. The purchase screen is protected by a parental gate. See the Amazon Privacy Notice for how Amazon handles purchases.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The app is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by collecting no data whatsoever from any user, child or adult.
